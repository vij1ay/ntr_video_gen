You are working inside a local development environment and must build a production-quality
local-first Nature Video Automation application.

    Natural-language/script input

10. SCENE DESIGN
============================================================

Relaxing videos should not change scenes too rapidly.

The LLM should select scene durations appropriate for the requested video.

For a 15-minute video, approximately:

    10-20 major scenes

is acceptable.

However, do not hard-code the number.

The scene planner should calculate this based on duration.

Scenes can be repeated where appropriate, but repetition should feel intentional.

Avoid obvious repetitive loops.

Use visual continuity.

For example:

    mountain
        ↓
    forest
        ↓
    river
        ↓
    waterfall
        ↓
    forest
        ↓
    mountain
        ↓
    sunset

rather than random unrelated scenes.

12. IMAGE / VIDEO GENERATION STRATEGY
============================================================

The system should support three media generation modes:

    IMAGE
    VIDEO
    HYBRID

IMAGE:

    Generate a still image
    Apply slow pan/zoom/parallax effects with FFmpeg
    Use for scenes that don't require complex movement

VIDEO:

    Generate a short AI video clip through ComfyUI
    Extend its usefulness through slow motion, looping or interpolation where appropriate

HYBRID:

    Use image + short motion clip + FFmpeg effects

The system should favor efficiency.

Do not generate unnecessary AI video.

For a relaxing nature video, subtle motion is sufficient.

Examples:

    trees moving slightly
    clouds moving slowly
    water flowing
    rain falling
    grass moving in wind
    animal making subtle movements
    camera slowly moving forward
    camera slowly panning

Avoid excessive camera movement.
